The problem

Avocado seedling (miche) nurseries track seed procurement and seedbeds by hand.

What it does

  • Seed procurement tracking for the nursery.
  • Seedbed and seedling management in one place.
  • Currently in active development.

How it's built

Full-stack web app deployed on Vercel.
