Church Management System

A role-based management system for a Seventh-day Adventist church, used by its offices to manage members, departments and giving.

Spring BootReactRole-based access
Live demo ↗

The problem

Church offices juggle members, giving, and departments on paper and spreadsheets.

What it does

  • Role-based access for Elders, Treasurers, Deacons and Clerks, each with the screens their office needs.
  • Member, department and giving records, with create, edit and delete.
  • Ledger periods and reports for the church's records.

How it's built

Spring Boot backend and a React frontend, with role-based access control.
