Genious Stock

Small shops lose money to untracked stock and manual sales records. Genious Stock replaces that with one system for inventory, sales, and reporting.

ReactInventoryPOSi18n
Live demo ↗

The problem

Shop owners often don't know exactly what is in stock, what sold today, or what is running low. Records are kept by hand, so mistakes and losses go unnoticed until the end of the month.

What it does

  • Tracks stock levels and flags items that are running low.
  • Records sales at the counter through a point-of-sale screen.
  • Works in two languages, so staff can use the one they are comfortable with.
  • Produces reports that follow TRA (Tanzania Revenue Authority) requirements.

How it's built

A React front end, deployed on Vercel. Built and maintained from an Android phone (Termux and Ubuntu) and from a PC (VS Code), depending on where the work happens.
