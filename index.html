About

Code that solves real problems.

I'm Amos Zabron Mhema. I study Agricultural Economics and Agribusiness at Sokoine University of Agriculture (SUA), and I'm also working toward a Computer Science degree at University of the People. I run my own venture, GENIOUS-OPEX ENTERPRISES.

I believe the best software comes from understanding the problem first. That's why my projects focus on real operations: inventory, patient records, farm-to-market flows, and community management.

My setup is unusual: I build and maintain full-stack apps entirely from an Android device using Termux and Ubuntu, deploying backends to Render and frontends to Vercel and Netlify.
